Every bounty execution **must** follow exactly one of these two sequences.

1. **Counterparty** pre‑pays USDC to `payTo` and pastes the transaction hash in a `bounty:` issue, referencing this spec.

3. **Operator** delivers a **new file only** on `master` that exactly matches the spec.
4. **Counterparty** confirms delivery by replying with "ACK" within 24 hours.
5. Failure to ACK within 24 hours deems the delivery accepted automatically.
